# Quick Capture v4.2.0

Thank you to everyone who upvoted Quick Capture and helped it become the most upvoted plugin in the DMS Plugin Registry.

For the foreseeable future, there will be no minor or major version bumps. Only patch releases will be published when necessary.

The next major effort is a complete rewrite of the screenshot backend to improve performance and responsiveness. In real-world testing, the new backend is already around twice as fast and noticeably smoother.

This rewrite is a substantial undertaking, so updates may be less frequent for a while. Thank you for your patience and support.
